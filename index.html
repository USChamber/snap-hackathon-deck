Weeks of handoffs → days of directed loops

The shape is the argument: the old way is a line — work moves one direction through six pairs of hands, and a mistake at the end restarts the whole line. The new way is a loop — prompt, generate, review, refine, around again in hours, and you only exit when it's right: ship. Don't over-narrate; point at the spinning ring.
